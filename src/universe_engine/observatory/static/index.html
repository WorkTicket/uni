About this model

Cosmic expansion and linear growth use the engine's flat ΛCDM background. The web is a truncated Zel'dovich approximation, not an N-body run. Galaxies, stars and planets are procedurally generated from the seed, and nothing here claims to simulate star, planet or life formation. Early-universe visuals are schematic, with contrast exaggerated.
